Maqueen Plus v3: at T-junctions, go …

Block: maqueen_v3_mode_T

What is it for?

Chooses what the V3 robot does, during line following, when it reaches a T-junction (the line stops and goes left and right): turn left, turn right or stop.

How to use it?

Put this block in the ‘on start’ block, before starting the line follower.

Good to know

  • This block needs a Maqueen Plus V3 chassis: V1 and V2 chassis do not have this built-in line follower.
  • At a T, going straight on is impossible: only ‘left’, ‘right’ and ‘stop’ are offered.

Example

At start, the V3 robot turns right at T-junctions, then line following starts.
