Kitrobot v2: spin … speed … %

Block: kitrobot_pivoter

What is it for?

Makes the robot turn on the spot, clockwise (↻) or anticlockwise (↺), at the chosen speed.

How to use it?

Choose the direction, then type the speed, from 0 to 100 %.

Good to know

  • The robot keeps turning until another order: add a ‘wait’ block, then the ‘Kitrobot v2: stop the robot’ block.
  • For a ready-made quarter turn, use the ‘spin left’ and ‘spin right’ blocks.

Example

The robot spins on the spot at 50 % for one second, then stops.
