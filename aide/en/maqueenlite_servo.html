Maqueen Lite: servo … angle … °

Block: maqueenlite_servo

What is it for?

Puts the servomotor plugged into socket S1 or S2 of the Maqueen Lite robot at the chosen angle.

How to use it?

Choose the servomotor's socket: S1 or S2.

Type the angle, from 0 to 180°.

Good to know

  • The program does not wait for the servomotor to get there: add a ‘wait’ block.
  • The robot gets this order through the I2C bus (pins P19 and P20).

Example

Servomotor S1 goes to 90°, then to 0° one second later.
