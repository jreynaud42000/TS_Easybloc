radio: transmit power …

Block: radio_puissance

What is it for?

Sets the power the board sends its messages with, from 0 (shortest range) to 7 (longest range).

How to use it?

Put this block in the ‘on start’ block, after the ‘turn on the radio’ block.

Good to know

  • A low power limits the range: handy so that a board only receives the boards very close to it.
  • Without this block, the power is 6.

Example

The radio transmits at maximum power (7): the range is the largest.
