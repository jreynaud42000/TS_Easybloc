RGB LCD: … the display

Block: grove_lcdrgb_alimenter

What is it for?

Hides or shows again the characters of the RGB LCD screen, without clearing them.

How to use it?

Choose turn off to hide the text, turn on to show it again.

Good to know

  • The text is kept: it comes back as it was with ‘turn on’.
  • The lit background is controlled separately, with the ‘RGB LCD: … the backlight’ block.

Example

‘Hello’ is written at start, then the text blinks: one second visible, one second hidden.
