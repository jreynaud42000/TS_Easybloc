LCD: … the display

Block: grove_lcd_alimenter

What is it for?

Hides or shows again the characters of the 16x2 LCD screen, without clearing them.

How to use it?

Choose turn off to hide the text, turn on to show it again.

Good to know

  • The text is kept: it comes back as it was with ‘turn on’.
  • The backlight of this screen stays on: it cannot be controlled.

Example

‘Hello’ is written at start, then the text blinks: one second visible, one second hidden.
