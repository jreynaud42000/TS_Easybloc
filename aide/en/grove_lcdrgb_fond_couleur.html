RGB LCD: backlight …

Block: grove_lcdrgb_fond_couleur

What is it for?

Chooses the colour of the lit background of the RGB LCD screen from a list.

How to use it?

Choose the colour in the block's list.

Good to know

  • The colour black turns the background off.
  • To mix your own colour, use the ‘RGB LCD: backlight red … green … blue …’ block.

Example

The background turns blue, ‘Hello’ shows, then the backlight turns off two seconds later.
