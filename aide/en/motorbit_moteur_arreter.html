Motor:bit: stop …

Block: motorbit_moteur_arreter

What is it for?

Stops a motor of the Motor:bit board (M1 or M2), or both at once.

How to use it?

Choose motor M1, motor M2 or both motors.

Good to know

  • To stop both motors at once, choose both motors.

Example

Motors M1 and M2 each turn for two seconds at speed 150, one after the other.
