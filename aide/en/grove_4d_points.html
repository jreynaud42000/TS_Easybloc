… the colon

Block: grove_4d_points

What is it for?

Turns on or turns off the colon in the middle of the 4-digit display, for example to write a time (12:34).

How to use it?

Choose turn on or turn off.

Good to know

  • The colon keeps its state when another number is shown.

Example

The number 1234 shows with the colon on: 12:34.
