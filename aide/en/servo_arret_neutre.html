set neutral stop of servomotor … to …

Block: servo_arret_neutre

What is it for?

With ON, a speed of 0 % given to the continuous rotation servomotor stops it completely, instead of sending it the ‘middle position’ order.

How to use it?

Choose the servomotor's pin, then ON or OFF.

Put this block in the ‘on start’ block.

Good to know

  • Handy when the continuous rotation servomotor still turns slowly at 0 %.
  • At first, the neutral stop is OFF.

Example

The neutral stop is switched on, then a speed of 0 % stops the servomotor plugged into P0.
