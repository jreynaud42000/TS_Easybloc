SGP30 air quality: …

Block: grove_sgp30

What is it for?

Returns a measurement of air quality made by the Grove SGP30 sensor: the eCO₂ (in ppm) or the total VOC (in ppb).

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Choose the quantity: eCO₂ (ppm) or Total VOC (ppb).

Good to know

  • VOC are volatile organic compounds (solvents, perfumes, glues…); eCO₂ is an estimate of CO₂ calculated by the sensor, not a real CO₂ measurement.
  • During the first 15 seconds, the sensor warms up: it returns 400 ppm and 0 ppb.
  • The sensor plugs into an I2C port. In the simulator, set the values with the sliders of the ‘Peripherals’ table.

Example

A sad face shows when the VOC go above 500 ppb, a happy face otherwise.
