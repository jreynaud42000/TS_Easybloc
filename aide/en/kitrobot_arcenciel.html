Kitrobot v2: Rainbow

Block: kitrobot_arcenciel

What is it for?

Lights up all the LEDs of the robot's strip with the colours of the rainbow, spread from red to violet.

How to use it?

Put this block where you want the rainbow to light up, for example in the ‘on start’ block.

Good to know

  • The colours stay on until the next block that changes the LEDs.

Example

The rainbow lights up at start.
