LiDAR: get the avoidance data

Block: lidar_acquerir

What is it for?

Reads the LiDAR sensor and keeps its results: direction to follow, emergency signal and distances on the left, in front and on the right.

How to use it?

Put this block in the ‘forever’ block, before the blocks that read these results.

Good to know

  • The ‘direction to follow’, ‘emergency signal’ and ‘distance of the obstacle’ blocks read the last acquisition again, not the sensor live.
  • The 4x4 avoidance mode is needed (‘set the size’ block).

Example

The LiDAR is prepared in avoidance mode with a distance of 200 mm, then the advised direction shows every second.
