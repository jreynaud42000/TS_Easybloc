π

Block: math_constant

What is it for?

Returns a mathematical constant: π (pi), e, φ (golden ratio), √2, √½ or ∞ (infinity).

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Choose the constant in the block's list.

Good to know

  • π is about 3.14: it is used to calculate the circumference or the area of a circle (circumference = 2 × π × radius).

Example

At start, the value of π scrolls on the LED matrix.
