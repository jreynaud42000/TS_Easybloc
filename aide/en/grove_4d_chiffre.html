show the digit … at position …

Block: grove_4d_chiffre

What is it for?

Shows one digit at the chosen position of the 4-digit display, without touching the others.

How to use it?

Type the digit, from 0 to 9, then the position, from 0 to 3 (from left to right).

Good to know

  • A value outside 0 to 9 turns off this position.
  • A position outside 0 to 3 is brought back to 0 or 3.

Example

The digit 7 shows at position 2 (positions go from 0 to 3).
