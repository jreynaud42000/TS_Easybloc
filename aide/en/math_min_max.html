Block: math_min_max

What is it for?

Returns the smaller (min) or the bigger (max) of two numbers.

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Choose min or max, then attach the two numbers.

Good to know

  • ‘max of measurement and 0’ prevents a negative result: it never goes below 0.

Example

At start, the bigger of 3 and 7 (that is 7) shows.
