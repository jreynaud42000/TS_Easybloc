Kitrobot v2: flash the robot

Block: kitrobot_clignoter

What is it for?

Makes all the LEDs of the robot's strip flash once, in white.

How to use it?

Put this block in a loop with a ‘wait’ block to make the robot flash several times.

Good to know

  • The flash lasts 0.15 second, then all the LEDs of the strip turn off.

Example

The robot flashes once at start.
