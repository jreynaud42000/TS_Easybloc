Maqueen Plus v3: stop the current PID control

Block: maqueen_v3_pid_stop

What is it for?

Interrupts the precise movement or turn in progress on the V3 robot.

How to use it?

Put it after a ‘… cm’ or ‘turn … °’ block without ‘wait until finished’.

Good to know

  • This block needs a Maqueen Plus V3 chassis (wheels with encoder).

Example

The V3 robot sets off for 50 cm without waiting for the end; one second later, the movement is interrupted.
