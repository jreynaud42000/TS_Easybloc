DFR0548: stop …

Block: dfr_moteur_arreter

What is it for?

Stops a DC motor of the DFR0548 board, or all four at once.

How to use it?

Choose the motor (M1 to M4), or all motors.

Good to know

  • It only acts on the DC motors: the servomotors keep their position.

Example

Motor M1 turns clockwise for two seconds at speed 150, then stops.
