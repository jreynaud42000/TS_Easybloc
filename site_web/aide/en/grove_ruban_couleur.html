colour the whole strip …

Block: grove_ruban_couleur

What is it for?

Lights up all the LEDs of the RGB strip in the chosen colour.

How to use it?

Choose the colour in the block's list.

Good to know

  • The colour takes into account the brightness set with the ‘strip brightness to … %’ block.
  • Without the ‘set the RGB Strip on …’ block, the strip is taken on pin P1 with 16 LEDs.

Example

The whole strip lights up in red for two seconds, then turns off.
