stop the motor …

Block: maqueen_arreter

What is it for?

Stops the left motor, the right motor or both motors of the Maqueen Plus robot.

How to use it?

Choose the motor: left, right or both.

Good to know

  • It is the same as the ‘motor … to …’ block with speed 0.

Example

Both motors turn at speed 50 for two seconds, then stop.
