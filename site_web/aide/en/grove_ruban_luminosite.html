strip brightness to … %

Block: grove_ruban_luminosite

What is it for?

Sets the brightness of the RGB strip's colours, from 0 % (off) to 100 % (maximum).

How to use it?

Put this block before the blocks that colour the strip.

Good to know

  • The brightness applies to the colours sent afterwards: the LEDs already lit do not change until they are coloured again.
  • A lower brightness is easier on the eyes and uses less energy.

Example

A strip of 16 LEDs is set on P0, its brightness is set to 50 %, then it lights up in red.
