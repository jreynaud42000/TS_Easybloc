[Audio] stop music

Block: audio_arreter

What is it for?

Stops the sound being played.

How to use it?

Put it, for example, in a ‘when button … is pressed’ block to cut the sound on demand.

Good to know

  • If no sound is playing, it does nothing.

Example

Button A plays a giggle, button B stops the sound.
