Block: text_join

What is it for?

Returns a text made of several pieces put end to end: text, numbers, measurements…

This block returns a text (to show, or to compare with another text using the ‘=’ block of the Logic category).

How to use it?

Attach the pieces in the boxes, in order.

To add boxes, click the block's gear.

Good to know

  • The pieces are stuck together without spaces: add the spaces in your texts (‘Temp: ’ with a space at the end).

Example

At start, the board scrolls ‘Temp: ’ followed by the temperature.
