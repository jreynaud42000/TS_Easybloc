LED Bar on … LED no. … …

Block: grove_barre_led

What is it for?

Turns one LED of the Grove LED bar on or off, without touching the others.

How to use it?

Choose the bar's port, then type the number of the LED, from 1 to 10.

Choose on or off.

Good to know

  • A number outside 1 to 10 does nothing.
  • The bar uses two pins: those of the chosen port.

Example

The LEDs of the bar light up one after the other, like a chaser.
