set the accelerometer range …

Block: gamme_accelerometre

What is it for?

Sets the measuring range of the accelerometer: 1g, 2g, 4g or 8g.

How to use it?

Put it in the ‘on start’ block.

Choose a small range to measure small movements, a large range to measure strong shocks.

Good to know

  • A larger range measures stronger shocks, but with less precision.

Example

The accelerometer is set to 8g, then the measured strength shows every second.
