stop the servomotor …

Block: servo_arreter

What is it for?

Stops controlling the servomotor: the board no longer sends it orders.

How to use it?

Choose the servomotor's pin.

Good to know

  • A continuous rotation servomotor stops.
  • A positional servomotor stays where it is, but no longer holds its position: you can turn it by hand.

Example

The servomotor plugged into P0 turns at 50 % for two seconds, then stops.
