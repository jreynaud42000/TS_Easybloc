set the servomotor pulse width … to … µs

Block: servo_impulsion

What is it for?

Controls the servomotor directly with the length of the pulse it receives, in microseconds (µs).

How to use it?

Choose the servomotor's pin, then type the length.

Good to know

  • The length is brought back between 500 and 2500 µs: 500 µs is 0°, 1500 µs is the middle (90°), 2500 µs is 180°.
  • The board sends a pulse every 20 milliseconds.
  • For everyday use, the ‘set the servomotor angle’ block is simpler.

Example

A pulse of 1500 µs puts the servomotor plugged into P0 in the middle.
