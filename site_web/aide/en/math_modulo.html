remainder of … ÷ …

Block: math_modulo

What is it for?

Returns the remainder of the whole-number division of one number by another: the remainder of 17 ÷ 5 is 2 (17 = 3 × 5 + 2).

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Attach the number to divide, then the divisor.

Good to know

  • A number is even when the remainder of its division by 2 is 0.
  • The remainder comes back to 0 regularly: handy to do something every 5 turns, for example.

Example

At start, the remainder of 17 ÷ 5 (that is 2) scrolls.
