motion detected on …

Block: grove_pir

What is it for?

Returns true when the Grove motion detector (PIR sensor) sees a person or an animal moving in front of it, false otherwise.

This block returns a true/false answer (to plug straight into an ‘if’ block, or into a block from the Logic category).

How to use it?

Choose the pin where the detector is plugged in.

Put this block straight into an ‘if’ block.

Good to know

  • It senses the heat of a moving body: a cold object that moves does not trigger it.
  • After a movement, it stays true for a few seconds.
  • In the simulator, click the white lens of the detector in the wiring, or tick ‘motion detected’ in the ‘Peripherals’ table.

Example

The buzzer plugged into P1 beeps when the motion detector plugged into P0 sees something move.
