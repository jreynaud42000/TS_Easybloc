Kitrobot v2: stop the robot

Block: kitrobot_arreter

What is it for?

Stops both motors of the robot.

How to use it?

Put it after a movement block and a ‘wait’ block.

Good to know

  • The board stops driving the wheel servomotors: the robot stops.

Example

The robot moves forward at 50 % for two seconds, then stops.
