magnetic force (µT) …

Block: capteur_boussole_force

What is it for?

Returns the magnetic field measured by the board along the x, y or z axis, or its total strength, in microteslas (µT).

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Choose the axis, or strength for the three together.

Bring a magnet near the board: the value changes.

Good to know

  • In the simulator, this measurement does not change (it is always 0): try the program on the real board.

Example

The magnetic field along the x axis shows every second on the LED matrix.
