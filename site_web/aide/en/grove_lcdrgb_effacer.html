RGB LCD: clear

Block: grove_lcdrgb_effacer

What is it for?

Clears all the text of the RGB LCD screen.

How to use it?

Put it before writing a new text, so as not to keep leftovers of the old one.

Good to know

  • The background colour does not change.

Example

‘Hello’ shows for two seconds, then the screen clears.
