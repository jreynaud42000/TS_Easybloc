Maqueen Plus v3: real speed of the wheel … (cm/s)

Block: maqueen_v3_vitesse_reelle

What is it for?

Returns the real speed of the left or right wheel of the V3 robot, in centimetres per second, measured by the wheel encoder.

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Choose the wheel: left or right.

Good to know

  • This block needs a Maqueen Plus V3 chassis (wheels with encoder).
  • The real speed can differ from the requested speed: slippery floor, slope, load…

Example

The speed of the left wheel shows every second on the LED matrix.
