turn off the strip

Block: grove_ruban_effacer

What is it for?

Turns off all the LEDs of the RGB strip.

How to use it?

Put it where the strip must turn off.

Good to know

  • To turn off one LED only, use the ‘turn off LED no. …’ block.

Example

The whole strip lights up in red for two seconds, then turns off.
