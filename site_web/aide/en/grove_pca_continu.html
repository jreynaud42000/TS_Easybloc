16-servo driver: continuous servo no. … to … %

Block: grove_pca_continu

What is it for?

Turns the continuous rotation servomotor plugged into a channel of the 16-servo driver, at the chosen speed.

How to use it?

Type the channel number, from 0 to 15, then the speed, from −100 to 100 %: a negative number turns the other way, 0 stops.

Good to know

  • If the servomotor still turns slowly at 0 %, it needs adjusting: try a small value (1 or −1, for example).
  • The servomotor keeps turning until the next block that controls it.

Example

The continuous servomotor on channel 1 turns one way for two seconds, then the other way for two seconds.
